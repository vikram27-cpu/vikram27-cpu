A film, movie, or motion picture is a form of visual art that tells stories and communicates ideas through moving images.

Films can contain characters, events, music, dialogue and visual effects. They are commonly created for entertainment, education and storytelling.

History

Film has developed over many years. Modern filmmaking uses cameras, computers, sound recording and various visual technologies.
